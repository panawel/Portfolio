Overview

"Leumi Goodies" is a unique program that allows bank Leumi customers to accumulate "Goodies" on credit card purchases.

Accumulation of "Goodies" on credit card expenses begins when the expenses on the credit cards reach an amount of 2,000 ₪ per month, up to 8,000 ₪.

The "Leumi Goodies" application allows the redemption of accumulated "Goodies" for a wide range of benefits from various content realms, such as movies, concerts, shows, food, fashion, beauty and grooming, vacations and spas, gyms and country clubs, and more.
